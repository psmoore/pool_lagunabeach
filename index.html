Laguna Beach High School & Community Pool

The pool is closed for reconstruction. Work began in June 2026 and is planned to finish in about May 2027. Until then, these eight pools in neighboring cities are open for lap swim, lessons and water fitness.

Where can I swim today? This board answers from each pool's own schedule: who's open right now, for what, on how many lanes, until when, and how far from the Laguna Beach pool.

The nearby pools

  • 1Laguna Niguel Aquatics Center

    Laguna Niguel · 3.9 mi from the LBHS pool

    25-yard lap pool, 13 lanes, with diving boards; a warm (87°) teaching pool

    The city's list says: Weekdays: 6am-1pm & 7-8pm · Weekends: 9am-4pm · Swim lessons & water aerobics offered · $5 drop-in, $80/20 visits
    Not quite: Weekends are two different swims: lap swim 9am–1pm, then rec swim 1–4pm with the diving boards open.

  • 2Aliso Viejo Aquatic Center

    Aliso Viejo · 5.0 mi from the LBHS pool

    25-meter lap pool (lane count not published), wading pool and splash pad; the rec pool is summer-only

    The city's list says: Mon/Wed: 10am-3:30pm · Tue/Thu: 6:30am-3:30pm · Fri-Sun: 10am-5pm · Swim lessons, swim team & water aerobics offered · $8 drop-in, $246/year
    Not quite: The 3:30pm times are when lap swim stops, not when the pool closes: it's open to 6pm Mon–Thu, with the lanes going to the Flying Fish swim team from 4pm.

  • 3Montanoso Recreation Center

    Mission Viejo · 7.2 mi from the LBHS pool

    25-yard pool, 6 lanes

    The city's list says: Weekdays: 5:30am-7:45pm · Saturdays: 7am-6:45pm · Sundays: 7am-4:45pm · Water aerobics offered · $10 drop-in, $40/month
    Not quite: From noon to close, family rec swim shares the pool with lap swim. No page says how many lanes stay roped for laps.

  • 4Sierra Recreation Center

    Mission Viejo · 7.0 mi from the LBHS pool

    25-yard pool, 8 lanes, diving board

    The city's list says: Weekdays: 5:30am-7:45pm · Saturdays: 7am-6:45pm · Sundays: 7am-4:45pm · Water aerobics offered · $10 drop-in, $40/month
    Not quite: The pool has been closed since Sep 8 for replastering. The city gives only a tentative reopening "by Thanksgiving weekend".

  • 5Marian Bergeson Aquatic Complex

    Newport Beach · 8.6 mi from the LBHS pool

    One outdoor pool, 50 m × 8 lanes or 25 yd × 18 lanes (a directory's figures; the city publishes none)

    The city's list says: Weekdays: 6-9pm · Weekends: 7am-12pm · Swim lessons offered · $16/5 visits, $209/year
    Not quite: Weekends run 7am–2pm, not to noon.

  • 6Woollett Aquatics Center

    Irvine · 11.0 mi from the LBHS pool

    Two 50-meter pools and a 10-lane teaching pool

    The city's list says: Weekdays: 6am-2pm & 6-8pm · Weekends 8-10am · Swim lessons & water aerobics offered · $5 drop-in, $525/year

  • 7San Clemente Aquatics Center

    San Clemente · 11.3 mi from the LBHS pool

    50-meter competition pool (17 short-course lanes) and a 25-yard, 6-lane activity pool with slides

    The city's list says: Mon/Wed/Fri: 5:30am-3pm & 4:30-8pm · Tue/Thu: 5:30am-12pm & 4:30-8pm · Saturday: 9am-4pm · Swim lessons & water aerobics offered · $13 drop-in, $132/month, $732/year
    Not quite: Mon/Wed/Fri lap swim runs 5:30am–8pm with no midday break; the break is only Tue/Thu, noon to 4:30pm, for staffing.

  • 8Ole Hanson Beach Club

    San Clemente · 11.3 mi from the LBHS pool

    25-yard, 6-lane pool and a teaching pool, above the beach

    The city's list says: Mon-Thu: 4:30-6:30pm · Tue/Thu: 8am-12pm · Sunday: 12-4pm · Swim lessons offered · $13 drop-in, $132/month, $732/year
    Not quite: There is no Mon–Thu 4:30–6:30pm lap swim on any San Clemente page. Lap swim is Tue/Thu 8am–noon.

Pool Modernization Project

The Laguna Beach Unified School District's aging pool, which also serves as the city's only pool, reached the end of its 30-year lifespan. The new aquatic facility will have 16 swim lanes, new restrooms and changing areas for the community and the school district, shaded seating, and rooftop solar water heating. The district has a project page with a live construction camera.

What this replaces

  • A map of pins copied once. The city's map and printable PDF copy each pool's hours from one moment. By October, several were already wrong. Sierra has been closed since September 8. Newport Beach's weekends run to 2pm, not noon. Ole Hanson has no Mon–Thu evening lap swim. Montanoso offers no water aerobics this fall. Aliso Viejo's "3:30pm" is when lap swim stops, not when the pool closes.
  • Eight sites, six operators, eight formats. Getting the real hours means a monthly PDF (Newport Beach), Canva images (Aliso Viejo, Laguna Niguel), a Google Calendar of wedding closures (Aliso Viejo), lane sheets saved as pictures and labeled "Summer" (San Clemente), a swim-team handbook (Laguna Niguel) and FlippingBook brochures.
  • Prices without saying whose. Four pins quote a non-resident price with no label. That is the right price for a Laguna Beach swimmer, who is a non-resident at every one of these pools. But nothing says so, and each operator's own page leads with its resident price. Montanoso's $40/month also leaves out a $50 initiation fee.
  • "Scheduled to begin on June 8, and is planned to last 12 months." The city's page gives no year and still reads as future tense. The school district says construction began in June 2026 and ends in May 2027. Its own timeline also still says the pool would be under construction "from March to August 2026".
  • One schedule instead. When each pool's schedule is entered once, this page, the printable list and every pool's calendar update together. A pool that closes says so here the same day.
